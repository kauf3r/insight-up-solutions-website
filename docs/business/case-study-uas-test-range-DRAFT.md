## The client

An FAA-designated UAS test range on the California coast — a working airfield that
hosts drone flight testing, environmental monitoring obligations, coworking/flex
office space, and a small ops team wearing all of the hats.

The classic ops-heavy small business problem: bookings in one system, calendars in
another, flight logs in a third, environmental compliance on a clipboard, and the
same status questions answered by hand every day.

## What we built

**Operations dashboard.** One screen for the day's flight operations — worksheets,
reporting, and range status that used to live across spreadsheets and email threads.

**Booking & calendar automation.** Space and resource bookings (OfficeRnD) flow
automatically to Google Calendar, with a daily operations summary that combines
bookings with the day's flight schedule. No re-typing, no double-booking, no morning
scramble to figure out who's on the range.

**Environmental monitoring with incident escalation.** Sensor data is monitored
continuously; threshold events automatically open incidents and notify the right
person — turning a compliance obligation from periodic manual checks into an
always-on system.

**Wildlife-tracking integration.** Live wildlife tracking (EarthRanger) integrated
into range awareness, supporting the environmental stewardship the range operates
under.
